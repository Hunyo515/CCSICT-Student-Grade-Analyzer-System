Semester grade computation

Activity percentage = score ÷ highest possible score × 100. Component percentage = sum of activity percentages × activity weights. Semester percentage = sum of component percentages × component weights. Blank scores stay unrecorded and contribute zero until entered.

Numerical grade reference: ISU Student Manual, grading system. Weights are set per subject and section for this semester.
